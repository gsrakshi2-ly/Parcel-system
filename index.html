How it works

The ultrasonic sensor checks parcel presence and the water sensor checks for water exposure. The readings are processed by the Arduino and shown on the website.

⌁

Our technology

Arduino UNO, ultrasonic distance sensing, a water sensor, serial communication and a browser interface.

◇

Protection guide

Keep electronics dry, position the water sensor where exposure can be detected, and test the setup carefully before demonstrations.

Frequently asked questions

Does PAQSAFE track a parcel's delivery route?

No. This prototype monitors parcel presence and water exposure locally. It does not provide courier GPS tracking.

Does the website work without an ESP32?

Yes. The website can connect to the Arduino UNO over USB using Web Serial in a supported browser. Wi-Fi is not required for this connection.

Are the dashboard values real?

Values marked Demo are illustrative. Real readings appear only after a compatible serial connection is made.

Is this a commercial product?

No. PAQSAFE is a student-developed prototype for educational purposes.

THE STORY BEHIND THE PROJECT

About Us

Five students. One idea. A bigger purpose.

Why we started

We are a group of five high school students who began this project as part of a school assignment. At first, we were simply looking for an idea that would help us complete the project. But we wanted to go beyond making something just for grades — we wanted to build something useful.

After scrapping our original idea, we found a problem that felt worth addressing: parcels can be damaged when exposed to water. We decided to explore a passive protection and monitoring system that could help identify parcel presence and water exposure.

PAQSAFE began as an assignment, but along the way we found an interest in designing, testing and improving a solution. We have put a lot of effort into developing the concept while keeping affordability and accessibility in mind.
